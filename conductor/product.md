# Initial Concept
projectNEWM is a music streaming platform and marketplace that leverages Non-Fungible Token (NFT) technology and Decentralized Finance (DeFi) to empower artists. It enables direct distribution, monetization, and interaction between artists, fans, and investors, removing traditional middlemen from the music industry.

# Product Definition

## Target Users
- **Independent Artists:** Seeking to distribute their music, maintain ownership rights, and monetize their work directly.
- **Fans/Listeners:** Interested in streaming music and supporting their favorite artists through direct interaction and investments.
- **Investors:** Looking to purchase and trade music-related NFTs and participate in the growth of artists' careers.

## Key Features
- **Music Streaming:** A high-quality, cross-platform playback experience including search and playlist management.
- **Music Marketplace:** A secure environment for buying and selling NFT-based music rights.
- **Artist Dashboard:** Tools for artists to upload their tracks, manage their portfolio, and view performance analytics.
- **Wallet Integration:** Secure management of cryptocurrency and NFT assets within the application.

## Product Goals
- **Democratize Music Distribution:** Use decentralized technology to return control and profit to the creators.
- **Seamless Cross-Platform Experience:** Deliver a consistent and high-quality user experience on Android, iOS, and other supported platforms.
- **Secure and Transparent Marketplace:** Ensure all transactions and ownership rights are handled securely via blockchain technology.
- **Community-Driven Ecosystem:** Foster a direct and meaningful connection between artists and their community of fans and investors.

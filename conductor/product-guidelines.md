# Product Guidelines

## Tone and Voice
- **Empowering & Visionary:** The application should speak to users with a sense of inspiration and forward-thinking. Communications must emphasize the future of music, creator independence, and the transformative power of decentralized technology. We want our artists to feel in control and our fans to feel like pioneers in a new musical frontier.

## Visual Identity
- **Vibrant & Artistic:** The design should reflect the creativity of the music it hosts. Use bold colors, dynamic transitions, and unique visual elements that celebrate artistic expression.
- **Dark-Mode First:** Prioritize a dark aesthetic. This provides a premium, immersive feel suitable for music consumption and aligns with the visual expectations of modern crypto and NFT management tools.

## User Experience Principles
- **Immersive Playback:** The streaming experience should be the centerpiece, with seamless transitions and a focus on high-fidelity visual and auditory feedback.
- **Transparent Transactions:** Marketplace and wallet interactions must be clear, providing users with absolute certainty about their assets and ownership.
